Title: Quarterly Safety Standard Report

Introduction: This report explains the purpose of the safety review conducted for Q3 2026
across all operational sites managed by the eastern division team.

Scope: This document covers all warehouse and logistics operations within the eastern
division, including three regional distribution centers.

Methodology: Data was gathered using scheduled site visits, structured staff interviews,
and a review of incident logs from the past ninety days.

Findings: Several minor issues were observed in the storage area, primarily related to
signage and walkway clearance. No major safety violations were identified during the review.

Recommendations: We recommend upgrading the ventilation system by next quarter and adding
additional floor markings near the loading docks to improve visibility.

Conclusion: Overall compliance is satisfactory with a small number of minor improvements
needed before the next scheduled audit.
